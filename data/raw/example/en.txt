Last Saturday it was raining, so Anna decided to go to the city library. The library is in an old building near the river. She took an umbrella, a notebook and two pens.

At the entrance she met a librarian, an elderly woman with kind eyes. The librarian gave Anna a reader's card and showed her the reading room. There were about twenty tables in the room. On each table stood a lamp.

Anna wanted to find a book about the history of China. She asked the librarian for help. The woman brought her three books and a map of ancient China. One of the books was very thick; the other two were thin.

Anna sat down at a table by the window. Outside, the rain was still falling. She read for two hours and wrote down a few of the most interesting facts. The first book told the story of the Great Wall. The second book was about the invention of paper.

At noon Anna felt hungry. Near the library there is a small café. She ordered a cup of tea and a piece of apple pie. The tea was hot, and the pie was delicious.

After lunch, Anna returned to the library. She borrowed two books for a week. The librarian wrote the date on a card and smiled. "Come again next Saturday," she said. Anna went home with a bag full of books and an idea for a new story.
