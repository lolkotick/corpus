Dear Masha,

I am writing to you from Xi'an, an ancient city in the north-west of China. I arrived here three days ago with a group of students from our university. We are staying in a hotel near the old city wall.

Yesterday we visited the Terracotta Army. It is one of the most famous places in China. Thousands of clay soldiers stand in long rows, and every soldier has a different face. Our guide told us that the figures are more than two thousand years old.

In the evening we walked along the city wall. The wall is so wide that people ride bicycles on it! We rented three bikes and rode for an hour. The sunset over the roofs was beautiful.

The food here is amazing. I have already tried a bowl of hand-made noodles and a famous local snack, a kind of sandwich with meat. My friend Li bought a kilo of dried fruit for her family. Tomorrow we are going to a cooking class.

I have taken hundreds of photos, and I will show you all of them when I come back. I miss you and our long talks in the café on Pushkin Street.

With love,
Katya
