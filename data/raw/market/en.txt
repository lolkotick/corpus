Every Saturday morning my grandmother goes to the market in the centre of the city. Last week I went with her for the first time. The market was noisy and full of colour.

First we stopped at a fruit stall. An old man was selling apples, pears and oranges. My grandmother bought two kilos of apples and a bunch of bananas. The man gave me an orange as a present.

Then we went to the vegetable section. There were baskets of potatoes, carrots and cabbages everywhere. Grandmother chose three cabbages and a few onions for soup.

Near the exit there was a small shop that sold tea. The owner, a woman from Yunnan, offered us a cup of green tea. It tasted fresh and slightly sweet. Grandmother bought a box of tea for my uncle.

On the way home we carried two heavy bags. I was tired, but happy. Now I understand why my grandmother loves the market: it is not only a place for shopping, but also a place for conversations.
