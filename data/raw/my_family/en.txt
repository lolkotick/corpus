My name is Tom. I am ten years old. I live in a small town with my family. We have a house with a red roof and a big garden.

There are five people in my family: my father, my mother, my two sisters and me. My father is a doctor. He works in a hospital. My mother is a teacher at a school near our house.

My older sister is fifteen. She likes music and plays the piano every evening. My younger sister is only four. She has a doll and a teddy bear.

We also have a dog and two cats. The dog is black and white. The cats sleep on the sofa all day.

On Sundays we have lunch together. My mother makes a big salad, and my father cooks fish. After lunch we walk in the park. I love my family very much.
